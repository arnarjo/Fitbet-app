## English (en-US)

**Name (30 chars):**
FitBet - Sports Fitness Bets

**Subtitle (30 chars):**
Bet. Lose. Work out.

**Description:**
FitBet is the social app where friends bet on sports outcomes — but no money is involved.

The loser must complete a fitness challenge. Run. Push-ups. Burpees. Cycling. You choose.

HOW IT WORKS
• Pick a match from Premier League, Champions League, Icelandic leagues and more
• Challenge a friend — predict the outcome
• Loser completes the challenge and submits photo or video proof
• Winner approves the proof

CHALLENGES
• Running (3, 5 or 10 km)
• Push-ups (25, 50 or 100)
• Squats (50, 100 or 200)
• Burpees, cycling, plank and more

LEAGUES & LEADERBOARD
• Create friend groups or workplace leagues
• Earn points with correct predictions
• Compete on the leaderboard

SUPPORTED COMPETITIONS
• Premier League
• UEFA Champions League
• Icelandic Premier Division (Besta deild)
• And more

STRAVA INTEGRATION
• Connect your Strava account
• Runs are automatically verified as completed challenges

No money. Just fun, competition, and fitness.

**Keywords:**
sports betting,fitness,friends,Premier League,challenge,workout,Strava,social,prediction

**Age Rating:** 12+

# PRIVACY POLICY lágmarkskröfur
# ══════════════════════════════════════════════

Þarf að geyma á https://fitbet.is/privacy og innihalda:

1. Hvaða gögn eru safnað:
   - Notandanafn, netfang, lykilorð (dulkóðað af Supabase)
   - Myndir/myndbönd úr áskorunum (geymd í Supabase Storage)
   - Push notification token (til tilkynninga)
   - Strava aðgangslykill (ef notandi tengir)

2. Hvernig gögn eru notuð:
   - Til að reka appið og senda tilkynningar
   - Aldrei seld til þriðja aðila
   - Aldrei notað til auglýsinga

3. Notandareglur:
   - Notendur geta eytt reikningi í appinu (Prófíll → Stillingar → Eyða reikningi)
   - Gögn eru eytt að fullu innan 30 daga

4. Hafðu samband:
   - support@fitbet.is
